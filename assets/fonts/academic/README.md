Inter and Source Serif 4 are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The accompanying OFL files are preserved with the fonts.

These are unmodified Latin and Latin Extended WOFF2 subsets served by Google Fonts.
Inter supplies regular and italic text; Source Serif 4 supplies the headings.
